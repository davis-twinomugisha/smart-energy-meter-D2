Electricity demand forecast

Software research prototype

Forecast next-hour consumption from 24 completed hourly readings. The examples replay public data from one household in France. Entered balances are scenarios. No utility balance or connected sensor is being read.

Forecast inputs

ISO date and time on the source clock, aligned to a whole hour.
Exactly 24 nonnegative values, oldest first. These are complete-hour energy totals, not instantaneous power.
Manually supplied. Forecast assumes no top-up during the next 24 hours.
